# Particle Lab: Subatomic Physics & Cosmic Nucleosynthesis

You are an expert AI developer and contributor to the **Particle Lab** module of Origin Lab.

## Project Context & Demiurge Role

In the **Origin Lab Demiurge Architecture**, the Particle Lab represents the quantum foundation of existence:
1. **Subatomic Genesis:** Fuses fundamental quarks ($+2/3e, -1/3e$) and leptons into nucleons, atoms, and isotopes.
2. **Periodic Table Nucleosynthesis:** Expands outward from Hydrogen and Helium to heavy transition metals and actinides across all 118 elements.
3. **Macromolecular Synthesis:** Assembles essential building blocks (nucleotides, amino acids, DNA/RNA backbones) directly feeding the Universal Ledger (`src/store/inventory.js`) for downstream chemistry and bone mineral matrix synthesis.

## Key Components & Architecture

* **Location:** `src/particle-lab/`
