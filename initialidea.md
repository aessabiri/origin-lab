# Project Singularity: The Genesis & Demiurge Simulation
**Core Vision & Game Design Document**

## 🌌 Overview
This project is an ambitious **Multi-Scale Universe & Life Simulator**.  
The player operates as the **Cosmic Demiurge (Architect of Reality)**, reversing the standard passive simulation loop:
1. **The Vision (Macro):** You design the ultimate intelligent organism in the **3D Anatomy Constructor** (all 206 individual bones, musculature, beating heart, and neural connectome).
2. **The Encoded Seed (Micro):** The system compiles your 3D design into an encrypted **Genetic Blueprint (Panspermia Seed / LUCA)**.
3. **The Living Reality (Cosmic):** The **Universe evolves in the background**, with stellar fusion and supernovae forging the exact elements ($Ca, P, Fe$) needed to materialize your blueprint.
4. **The Deployment:** You inoculate primordial Earth with your seed and guide Darwinian selection to unfold the body you designed.
5. **The Cosmic Ouroboros:** When self-awareness ignites, the loop closes — the observer understands the universe and restarts the Singularity.

## 🔗 Core Rules & Architectural Tenets
* **Unified Universal Ledger:** All elements and molecules share a single global store (`useInventory`).
* **Astrophysical Gating:** You cannot build bones without Calcium ($Ca$), and Calcium only comes from dying massive stars in the background universe.
* **Biophysical Authenticity:** Real 64 codons, real Ramachandran protein angles, real 206 human bones, real closed-chain posture mechanics.
* **Zero Cognitive Friction:** Clean, OLED-dark aesthetic, atomic micro-actions, zero AI-slop, and 100% verified test passes.
