# Project Singularity: The Genesis Simulation
**Initial Concept & Game Design Document**

## 🌌 Overview
This project is not just a science sandbox; it is a **Universe Evolution Simulator**.
The goal is to take the player on a journey from **before the Big Bang** all the way to the emergence of **Human Intelligence**.

The player acts as the "Architect of Reality," utilizing three specialized laboratories (Particle, Chemistry, Biology) to engineer the building blocks required to advance the universe through its cosmic phases.

---

## ⏳ Phase 1: The Void & The Big Bang
**"Before Time Began"**

*   **Visuals:** The game begins in a pre-Big Bang state. The screen visualizes "mysterious energies"—quantum fluctuations and void noise.
*   **Action:** The player interacts with these energies to trigger the singularity.
*   **The Event:** A cinematic transition simulates the **Big Bang**, followed by the expansion of space and the cooling of the universe into the **Quark-Gluon Plasma**.

## ⚛️ Phase 2: The Cosmic Forge (Particle Lab)
**"From Quarks to Stars"**

*   **Core Loop:** The player enters the **Particle Lab** to construct the fundamental matter of the universe.
*   **Progression Mechanic:**
    *   The Universe Timeline is "gated" by material discoveries.
    *   *Example:* To advance from **Galaxy Formation** to **Star Formation**, the player must synthesize elements up to **Oxygen** in the Particle Lab.
*   **Gameplay:**
    *   Fusion of Quarks → Protons/Neutrons.
    *   Nucleosynthesis of Atoms (Hydrogen → Helium → Carbon → Oxygen).
    *   These created elements are "exported" to the Universe view to fuel the formation of stars, nebulae, and eventually, the Solar System.

## ⚗️ Phase 3: The Molecular Era (Chemistry Lab)
**"The Ingredients of Life"**

*   **Setting:** Solar System Formation & Earth Formation.
*   **Core Loop:** With the Earth cooling, the player moves resources from the Particle Lab to the **Chemistry Lab**.
*   **Gameplay:**
    *   **NileRed Style Reactions:** Realistic, visual chemical simulations.
    *   **Goal:** Synthesize complex organic molecules from raw elements.
    *   **Key Synthesis:** Creating Amino Acids, RNA, and DNA structures.
    *   These molecules are the "inventory" required for the next phase.

## 🧬 Phase 4: The Architect of Life (Biology Lab)
**"Designing the Seed"**

*   **Setting:** The Primordial Earth.
*   **Core Loop:** Use the organic molecules created in the Chemistry Lab to construct the **First Living Cell**.
*   **Gameplay:**
    *   **Protein Folding Mechanic:** Visualizing and coding the actual folding of proteins to create functional organelles.
    *   **Cell Assembly:** Piecing together the membrane, mitochondria, and nucleus.
    *   **The Goal:** successfully create **LUCA** (Last Universal Common Ancestor).

## 🌍 Phase 5: Planetary Evolution (Earth View)
**"From Cell to Civilization"**

*   **Setting:** The Living Earth (God-Game Mode).
*   **Action:** The player plants their custom-built LUCA into Earth's oceans.
*   **Gameplay:**
    *   **Climate Control:** The player does not control individual animals. Instead, they control the **Environment**.
    *   **Weather & Atmosphere:** Manipulate temperature, oxygen levels, and weather patterns to drive Natural Selection.
    *   **Evolution:** Guide the lifeforms through evolutionary epochs (e.g., leaving the ocean, surviving ice ages).
*   **Winning Condition:** The successful evolution of a **Human** capable of understanding the universe that created it.

---

## 🔗 Core Mechanics
*   **Unified Inventory:** Resources are shared across all phases. Atoms made in Phase 2 are used in Phase 3; Molecules made in Phase 3 are used in Phase 4.
*   **Interconnected Labs:** You cannot skip steps. Biology requires Chemistry; Chemistry requires Physics.
*   **Visual Fidelity:**
    *   Accurate Particle Physics (Standard Model).
    *   Realistic Chemical Reactions (Phases, Thermodynamics).
    *   Detailed Biological Folding (Proteins/DNA).
    *   Cinematic Universe Rendering (Big Bang, Galaxies).
